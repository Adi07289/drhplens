## Decisions Made

- **Discriminated union over subclass** (RESEARCH caveat e / A3): `Claim | ToolClaim`, not `FusedClaim(Claim)`. A `Claim` subclass inherits DRHP-span-shaped required fields (`drhp_page`, `verbatim_span`, `span_offsets`, `sources` min_length=1); forcing a peer/forecast number through it would fabricate a DRHP grounding it does not have. The sibling model is the honest shape and leaves the locked `Claim` contract untouched.
- **`ToolClaim.source_tool` Literal is the three read-only tools** (`query_peers`, `query_forecast`, `query_redflags`) per the plan — GMP folds into `query_forecast` (D-04), never its own claim source.
- **P8 numeric values are starting calibration points** (RESEARCH Open Q1), documented as discretion values tuned against the D-09 stress fixtures; the semantics are what D-06 locks.

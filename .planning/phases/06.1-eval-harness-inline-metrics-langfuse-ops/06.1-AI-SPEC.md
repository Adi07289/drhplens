## 1. System Classification

**System Type:** Hybrid — a RAG **evaluation + observability harness** layered over the existing DRHPLens LangGraph RAG agent (LLM-as-judge faithfulness + deterministic retrieval metrics + trace enrichment).

**Description:**
An offline, committed evaluation suite for the DRHPLens RAG agent. It computes three metrics over a committed gold set — RAG **faithfulness** (DeepEval LLM-judge via Gemini, *reported*), retrieval **recall@k** (k=5/10/30, deterministic span-overlap, *gated*), and **citation-accuracy** (deterministic span-overlap, *gated*) — commits them to `eval/reports/eval_summary.json` + a dated markdown report, surfaces an honest **system-level** figure inline on IPO pages (per-IPO only where a real gold set exists), and enriches Langfuse traces with cost/latency/tool-call counts plus a failure-mode taxonomy as custom scores. Users: the **developer** (Langfuse ops review) and **recruiters/retail users** (honest inline metrics). "Good" = metrics are real, reproducible, honestly presented (no evaluation theater), and actually gate releases.

**Critical Failure Modes:**
1. **Evaluation theater (P10)** — a headline metric shown without an interpretation paragraph / failure analysis, or a metric that is reported but does not actually gate anything.
2. **Fabricated or misleading inline numbers** — showing a per-IPO number for an IPO with no real gold set, or any red/green / badge / verdict styling on an eval figure (violates the SEBI honesty invariant).
3. **Non-reproducible or flaky gate** — the release gate blocking CI on the non-deterministic LLM-judge, or the committed report and the gate computing divergent numbers.
4. **Agent answers without retrieving (P18)** — faithfulness not grounded in retrieved context; traces failing to capture the retrieval step.
5. **Observability coupling breaks base runs** — Langfuse enrichment failing to no-op when keys are unset (local dev / CI without secrets).

## 1b. Domain Context

> Researched by `gsd-domain-researcher`. Grounds the evaluation strategy in domain expert knowledge.

**Industry Vertical:** Finance — Indian primary-market (IPO) equity research; specifically retail-facing analysis of SEBI DRHP/RHP prospectuses. The system under evaluation is a document-grounded RAG agent; this harness judges whether that agent's cited answers are trustworthy.

**User Population:** Two lenses. (1) **Indian retail investors** (mobile-first, non-expert) who read the inline eval figure as a trust signal before acting on a cited DRHP answer. (2) A **DS/ML recruiter** reviewing the portfolio, who reads the harness *itself* as evidence of rigor (real metrics, reproducibility, no evaluation theater). The domain experts who **calibrate** the rubric are financial analysts (prospectus readers) + DS eval practitioners + a SEBI-compliance lens.

**Stakes Level:** High — real money decisions on regulated securities during a live IPO subscription window, but the product is explicitly **informational/educational, NOT investment advice** (deliberately outside SEBI RA/RIA scope). Stakes are high on faithfulness and numeric fidelity, and high on *how* results are surfaced (the honesty invariant) — not on producing a buy/sell verdict, which the product must never do.

**Output Consequence:** A retail investor may subscribe / not subscribe to an IPO partly informed by a cited DRHP answer; a wrong number (crore vs lakh), a mis-attributed related-party transaction, or a confidently-wrong risk summary can push a real financial decision on a fabricated fact. Downstream of the harness specifically: a dishonest or theatrical eval figure would launder an untrustworthy answer as trustworthy — the exact failure this harness exists to prevent.

### What Domain Experts Evaluate Against

**Dimension: Groundedness / faithfulness (every claim traceable to a retrieved DRHP span)**
- Good (expert accepts): every number and factual claim in the answer is supported by the top-k reranked DRHP chunks the agent *actually retrieved* — nothing paraphrased into existence.
- Bad (expert flags): a plausible-sounding claim absent from the retrieved context — fabricated statistic, invented risk, or "answered from prior knowledge" (the P18 answer-without-retrieving mode).
- Stakes: Critical
- Source: Stanford–Databricks study of 12 production RAG pipelines (legal/health/finance) found even the best metric — LLM-judge faithfulness — missed ~1 in 3 factual errors; a deployed financial RAG showed a ~21.9% hallucination rate. Financial-RAG practice sets faithfulness thresholds at 0.95+ for high-stakes (financial) domains.

**Dimension: Citation correctness (the cited page actually contains the claim)** — the project's custom "citation accuracy"
- Good: the cited page/span resolves to a DRHP location that genuinely contains the stated claim (deterministic span overlap).
- Bad: correct fact, wrong attribution — cites the right section ("Risk Factors") but the wrong page, or a page that does not contain the claim. "Citation hallucination" manufactures false confidence.
- Stakes: Critical
- Source: financial-RAG citation-precision research (claim-extraction errors, NLI domain mismatch, citation hallucination); CLAUDE.md project rule — "did the cited page actually contain the claim" is the custom metric, and a citation-accuracy regression is a hard CI failure.

**Dimension: Numeric fidelity (units + magnitude on Indian financial figures)**
- Good: figures preserve the DRHP's units and scale — lakh vs crore, % of revenue, subscription ×-times — exactly as filed, for the right fiscal period.
- Bad: number transposition, a unit slip (crore reported as lakh or vice versa), or retrieving the prior-year figure for a current-year question. A wrong number is worse than an honest hedge.
- Stakes: Critical
- Source: financial-LLM research — GPT-4 answered SEC-filing numeric questions correctly only 19–89% *even given the exact document*; near-identical embeddings for "Q3 2024 revenue ₹4.2B" vs "Q3 2023 revenue ₹4.2B" cause period-retrieval collisions. CLAUDE.md mandates tables as a *separate structured index path*, not embedded as flat text.

**Dimension: Retrieval completeness on long, repetitive prospectus boilerplate (recall vs precision)**
- Good: the reranked window surfaces the *specific* span(s) that answer the question, despite the 400-page DRHP repeating similar risk-factor / legal boilerplate across many pages.
- Bad: the answer draws on generic boilerplate rather than the specific disclosure; the true source span sits outside top-k — a recall miss the *deterministic* recall@k must catch (never papered over by feeding the judge extra context the agent never saw).
- Stakes: High
- Source: CLAUDE.md RAG-stack notes — "long, repetitive boilerplate sections — recall is easy, precision is hard"; cross-encoder reranking is the mitigation; recall tracked at k = 5/10/30.

**Dimension: Honest, non-prescriptive presentation (the compliance / UX lens)**
- Good: a figure is shown with full provenance (n, IPO, date, judge model) + a link to the committed report; plain IBM-Plex-Mono numbers; a low score and a high score render *identically*; every figure paired with an interpretation, not a verdict.
- Bad: red/green, badges, severity icons; a headline metric with no interpretation paragraph; a metric "reported" that gates nothing (evaluation theater, P10); any phrasing that reads as a buy/sell recommendation.
- Stakes: High
- Source: SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations + the Jan 8 2025 RA Guidelines (informational/educational boundary; AI-tool-use disclosure); the project's honesty invariant (P10 evaluation-theater avoidance).

### Known Failure Modes in This Domain

1. **Number transposition / unit error** (crore vs lakh, % of revenue) — a wrong magnitude on a financial figure; worse than a hedge. Rooted in table-extraction and temporal-retrieval fragility.
2. **Right section, wrong page** — the citation points to the correct DRHP section but a page that does not contain the claim (citation hallucination / mis-attribution).
3. **Confident answer on boilerplate risk-factor language** — the agent synthesizes generic prospectus boilerplate into a specific-sounding claim; a precision failure masked by fluent prose.
4. **Related-party-transaction (RPT) / promoter mis-attribution** — attributing an RPT or a promoter fact to the wrong entity/party; high-signal for retail due diligence, and easy to get subtly wrong.
5. **Answering without retrieving (P18)** — the agent (or the judge) produces an answer not grounded in the retrieved context; faithfulness must be judged against *exactly* what the agent retrieved, or the score inflates.
6. **Evaluation theater (P10)** — the harness's own failure mode: a metric shown without interpretation/failure analysis, or reported-but-non-gating, laundering an untrustworthy answer as trustworthy.

### Regulatory / Compliance Context

SEBI (India) is the binding regime — this is **not** SEC/US; DRHP/RHP are SEBI filings and figures are in lakh/crore, not USD millions. Do not import US-filing assumptions.

- **SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014** as amended 16 Dec 2024, plus the **RA Guidelines circular of 8 Jan 2025**: define who is a "research analyst" and constrain investment recommendations. DRHPLens deliberately stays informational/educational to remain outside RA/RIA scope — it must never emit a buy/sell/subscribe recommendation, price target, or verdict.
- **Jan 2025 AI-tool-use disclosure:** RAs must disclose the extent of AI-tool use. The product framing (honest, cited, AI-methodology-visible) is aligned; naming the judge model in every eval figure is part of that disclosure posture (provenance, not decoration).
- **Honesty invariant (load-bearing on HOW metrics surface):** no red/green, no badges, no severity/verdict UX, no prescriptive language; a low score and a high score must render identically; every figure carries provenance + a report link. This constrains the eval-surface *design* (P10 + honest presentation), not just the answer content.

### Domain Expert Roles for Evaluation

| Role | Responsibility in Eval |
|------|------------------------|
| Financial analyst / DRHP reader (domain SME) | Author + label the gold set (question → expected page-span sources → expected figure); adjudicate numeric-fidelity and RPT-attribution edge cases; define what "the cited page contains the claim" means per question type. |
| DS / ML eval practitioner (the developer) | Calibrate the DeepEval LLM-judge against human faithfulness labels (target ≥ 0.7 correlation) before trusting it; own the deterministic recall@k / citation-accuracy implementation, the release-gate thresholds, and Langfuse ops review. |
| SEBI-compliance reviewer (the compliance lens) | Enforce the honesty invariant on the eval surface (no verdict/severity UX, no prescriptive language); verify the informational/educational boundary and AI-disclosure posture. (Becomes the formal SEBI legal-review gate in 6c.) |

For this portfolio project these roles collapse onto the developer wearing three hats (analyst, DS, compliance). The rubric above is the artifact that keeps each hat honest, and the ≥ 50-example human spot-check is the non-negotiable calibration step before any figure is surfaced.

## 2. Framework Decision

**Selected Framework:** **DeepEval** (LLM-as-judge, for the faithfulness dimension only) + deterministic in-repo metrics for recall@k & citation-accuracy, on the existing stack (LangGraph agent, Langfuse tracing, Gemini judge). RAGAS = optional offline cross-check only.

**Version:** DeepEval — pin latest at spike (not yet a dependency); Gemini 2.5 Flash as judge model; Langfuse (already a dependency, plumbing live in `app/observability/`). `gsd-ai-researcher` to confirm the exact DeepEval pin + Gemini-as-custom-judge wiring.

**Rationale:**
DeepEval is CI-native (pytest `assert_test`), which directly satisfies the roadmap's "DeepEval CI integration" spike flag and is a recognizable portfolio signal. It is used **only** for the faithfulness LLM-judge dimension. Recall@k and citation-accuracy stay **deterministic span-overlap** metrics (already implemented in `scripts/run_eval.py`) because for span-level DRHP eval "correct source = a page range" is exactly measurable — deterministic is more honest and reproducible than an LLM-judged `context_recall`, and it is safe to hard-gate. The report and the release gate import ONE metric implementation (mirrors the existing `compute_numeric_faithfulness` that `scripts/release_gate.py` already imports).

**Alternatives Considered:**

| Framework | Ruled Out Because |
|-----------|------------------|
| RAGAS (as primary suite) | Already half-wired, but `context_recall` is LLM-judged and needs reference answers — noisier than the existing deterministic recall@k, and it has no CI-native assertion API. Retained as a flag-gated **offline cross-check** only. |
| Custom-only (no framework) | Most reproducible/cheapest, but loses the LLM-judge faithfulness dimension and the DeepEval/RAGAS portfolio keyword; the deterministic pieces are kept, so this collapses into the chosen hybrid. |
| TruLens | Best observability UX but smaller 2026 community traction; RAGAS+DeepEval is the more recognizable resume pair; Langfuse already covers observability. |

**Vendor Lock-In Accepted:** Partial — DeepEval is used for a single dimension and is swappable; the deterministic metrics are framework-free; Langfuse Cloud (free tier) hosts tracing with a no-op fallback so the agent runs without it.

### Entry Point Pattern
```python
import os
from deepeval.models import GeminiModel
from deepeval.metrics import FaithfulnessMetric
from deepeval.test_case import LLMTestCase

# 1. Custom judge = Gemini 2.5 Flash. temperature=0 for max determinism (Pitfall 3).
judge = GeminiModel(
    model="gemini-2.5-flash",
    api_key=os.environ["GEMINI_API_KEY"],
    temperature=0.0,
)

# 2. One test case per gold question. retrieval_context = the reranked chunks the
#    agent ACTUALLY saw — this grounds faithfulness in retrieval and guards P18.
test_case = LLMTestCase(
    input=question,                                   # the gold-set question
    actual_output=grounded_answer.answer_prose,       # the agent's prose
    retrieval_context=[c["payload"]["chunk_text"] for c in reranked_top_k[:5]],
)

# 3. threshold = the REPORTED target line (0.95). It renders "target", it does NOT fail CI.
metric = FaithfulnessMetric(threshold=0.95, model=judge, include_reason=True, async_mode=False)

# 4. measure() records score + reason WITHOUT raising — this is the report-not-gate path.
metric.measure(test_case)
faithfulness = metric.score        # float 0..1 -> eval_summary.json["aggregate"]["faithfulness_deepeval"]
reason       = metric.reason       # judge rationale -> committed into the .md report
# assert_test(test_case, [metric]) would RAISE below threshold — reserve it for the opt-in pytest lane.
```

### Key Abstractions
| Concept | What It Is | When You Use It |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| `GeminiModel` (`deepeval.models`) | Native DeepEval judge backed by `google-genai`; takes `model`, `api_key`, `temperature` | The judge for `FaithfulnessMetric` — replaces DeepEval's OpenAI default (free-tier constraint) |
| `LLMTestCase` | Carries `input` / `actual_output` / `retrieval_context` (+ optional `expected_output`) | One per gold question; `retrieval_context` must equal the reranked window the agent saw |
| `FaithfulnessMetric` | LLM-judge: fraction of answer claims supported by `retrieval_context` | `.measure()` = reported (no raise); `assert_test` = gated (raises) |
| `metric.measure()` vs `assert_test()` | `measure` records `score`/`reason`; `assert_test` raises `AssertionError` below threshold | `measure` → runner + `eval_summary.json`; `assert_test` → opt-in, non-blocking pytest lane |
| `langfuse.create_score(trace_id=, name=, value=, data_type=)` | v3/v4 API to attach a numeric/categorical custom score by trace id | Failure-mode + faithfulness scores on a trace from OUTSIDE the span context (replaces v2 `client.score`) |

### Common Pitfalls
1. **Langfuse v2 → v3/v4 API break (the big one).** A fresh `pip install langfuse` resolves to 4.14.x (OTEL SDK). The repo's `app/observability/` is v2: `from langfuse.callback import CallbackHandler`, `from langfuse.decorators import langfuse_context`, and `client.score(trace_id=..., name=..., value=..., comment=...)` — **all three are gone in v3+.** Migrate: `from langfuse.langchain import CallbackHandler` (its constructor takes **no** `public_key`/`secret_key` in v3+ — auth is via `Langfuse()`/env); `langfuse_context.update_current_trace(...)` → `get_client().update_current_trace(...)` inside a span, or `create_score(trace_id=...)` outside one; `client.score(...)` → `langfuse.create_score(trace_id=..., name=..., value=..., data_type="NUMERIC"|"CATEGORICAL", comment=...)`. Either pin `langfuse<3` to keep the existing v2 code, or (recommended for a 2026 portfolio) migrate the three helper files. Do NOT assume the current imports still resolve.
2. **DeepEval defaults to OpenAI and will 401 / bill you.** Every metric silently uses OpenAI unless you pass `model=GeminiModel(...)` (or run `deepeval set-gemini`). Always pass the judge explicitly to `FaithfulnessMetric(model=judge)` — the repo has no OpenAI key and must not acquire one.
3. **The LLM-judge is non-deterministic → never hard-gate it.** Even at `temperature=0`, Gemini faithfulness drifts run-to-run (claim segmentation + verdict sampling). Set `temperature=0`, run the pytest lane with `-c` (cache) and `-i` (ignore-errors, so malformed judge JSON doesn't fail the build), and keep faithfulness REPORTED. The deterministic `citation_accuracy` / `recall@10` are what gate — hard-gating the judge is exactly the "non-reproducible / flaky gate" critical failure mode.
4. **Gemini free-tier rate limits throttle the judge.** Free-tier `gemini-2.5-flash` is ~1500 req/day (per CLAUDE.md) with a tight per-minute ceiling. `FaithfulnessMetric` fans out multiple judge calls per test case (claim extraction + per-claim verdict), so 13 questions ≈ dozens of calls. `async_mode=True` (the default) bursts concurrent calls → 429s; on free tier prefer `async_mode=False` (or `deepeval test run -n 1`), add `tenacity` backoff, and cache with `-c`.
5. **RAGAS 0.4 API drift (keep offline only).** The existing `run_eval.py::_ragas_faithfulness` uses the pre-0.3 API (`from ragas.metrics import faithfulness`, `Dataset.from_dict({"question","answer","contexts"})`) — broken on 0.4.3, which now uses `Faithfulness` (class), `SingleTurnSample(user_input=, response=, retrieved_contexts=)`, an explicit `LangchainLLMWrapper` / `LlamaIndexLLMWrapper` evaluator LLM, and `EvaluationDataset`. Keep RAGAS flag-gated and out of CI precisely so this drift can never break the release path.
6. **v3/v4 scores need an explicit `flush()`.** Short-lived scripts (the eval runner, CI) must call `langfuse.flush()` (or `get_client().flush()`) before exit, or traces/scores are silently dropped. The OTEL SDK batches in the background; process exit races the flush.

## 4. Implementation Guidance

**Model Configuration:**
- **Judge:** `gemini-2.5-flash` via `deepeval.models.GeminiModel`, `temperature=0.0`, `include_reason=True`, `async_mode=False` (free-tier: avoid 429 bursts), `strict_mode=False`. Free-tier Gemini only — no OpenAI. The judge name is committed into `eval_summary.json["judge_model"]` for provenance (honesty invariant: never show a figure without naming the judge).
- **FaithfulnessMetric:** `threshold=0.95` — a **reported target line only**, never a gate. Read `metric.score` after `metric.measure()`.
- **Deterministic metrics** (`recall_at_k`, `citation_accuracy`): **no model** — pure functions over page-range span overlap. These are what the release gate blocks on.

**Core Pattern:** one metric implementation, two consumers (report + gate); `measure()`-not-`assert_test()` for faithfulness.
```python
# eval/metrics/faithfulness_deepeval.py — REPORTED, never raises on a low score
import os
from functools import lru_cache
from deepeval.models import GeminiModel
from deepeval.metrics import FaithfulnessMetric
from deepeval.test_case import LLMTestCase

@lru_cache(maxsize=1)
def _judge() -> GeminiModel:
    return GeminiModel(model="gemini-2.5-flash",
                       api_key=os.environ["GEMINI_API_KEY"], temperature=0.0)

def faithfulness_deepeval(question: str, answer: str, contexts: list[str]) -> tuple[float, str]:
    """Return (score, reason). Never raises on a low score — the caller REPORTS it.
    Infra failures (missing key / rate-limit exhaustion) propagate so the runner can
    record 'not measured' (-1 sentinel) rather than a misleading 0.0."""
    tc = LLMTestCase(input=question, actual_output=answer, retrieval_context=contexts)
    metric = FaithfulnessMetric(threshold=0.95, model=_judge(),
                                include_reason=True, async_mode=False)
    metric.measure(tc)              # measure() != assert_test(): records, does not raise
    return float(metric.score), (metric.reason or "")
```
```python
# eval/metrics/recall.py — GATED, deterministic, model-free (lifted from run_eval._recall_at_k)
def recall_at_k(expected_sources: list[dict], reranked_chunks: list[dict], k: int) -> float:
    if not expected_sources:
        return 1.0
    top_k, hits = reranked_chunks[:k], 0
    for exp in expected_sources:
        s, e = exp.get("page_start", 0), exp.get("page_end", 9999)
        for ch in top_k:
            p = ch.get("payload", {})
            if p.get("page_start", 0) <= e and p.get("page_end", 0) >= s:
                hits += 1
                break
    return hits / len(expected_sources)
```
`scripts/release_gate.py` imports these `eval/metrics` functions directly — the same way it already imports `compute_numeric_faithfulness` — so the committed report and the gate can never diverge.

**Tool Use:** The DeepEval Gemini judge is the **only** LLM call in the harness. Everything else — `recall_at_k`, `citation_accuracy`, and the existing `numeric_faithfulness` (via `agent.nodes.cite_check`) — is deterministic and offline. The agent under test is invoked through the existing `agent.graph.invoke_with_tracing(state, question)` to produce `reranked_top_k` + `grounded_answer`; no new agent tools are added. Langfuse enrichment (cost/latency/tool-call counts + failure-mode custom scores) attaches to that same run via the migrated `app/observability/` callbacks.

**State Management:** Two committed artifacts are the durable state: `eval/reports/eval_summary.json` (machine, pinned schema — read by both the UI and the gate) and `eval/reports/<date>-rag-eval.md` (human; judge `reason` strings live here). Faithfulness is stored as a value **or `-1` "not measured"** (rate-limited / key unset), never conflated with a real `0.0` — mirroring the existing `_ragas_faithfulness` `-1` sentinel. Per-IPO figures are written only for IPOs that have a real gold set (`per_ipo` map); absent that, no per-IPO number is emitted.

**Context Window Strategy:** The judge's `retrieval_context` = the **top-5 reranked chunks the generate node actually consumed** (already truncated to ~500 chars each in `run_eval.py`). Gemini 2.5 Flash's 1M-token window could fit the whole DRHP, but faithfulness must be judged against what the agent retrieved — feeding the judge more context than the agent saw would inflate the score dishonestly and defeat P18. Keep `retrieval_context` identical to the reranked window; do not summarize or expand it.

## 4b. AI Systems Best Practices

> Written by `gsd-ai-researcher`. Cross-cutting patterns every developer building AI systems needs — independent of framework choice.

### Structured Outputs with Pydantic

The committed `eval_summary.json` **is** the structured output — validate it with a Pydantic model at write time (this is the schema test the design doc pins as the UI contract):

```python
from pydantic import BaseModel, Field, field_validator

class Aggregate(BaseModel):
    faithfulness_deepeval: float = Field(ge=-1.0, le=1.0)  # -1 = not measured (report-only)
    citation_accuracy: float = Field(ge=0.0, le=1.0)       # GATED >= 0.95
    recall_at_5: float = Field(ge=0.0, le=1.0)
    recall_at_10: float = Field(ge=0.0, le=1.0)            # GATED >= 0.85
    recall_at_30: float = Field(ge=0.0, le=1.0)

class Corpus(BaseModel):
    gold_set: str
    ipo: str
    n_questions: int = Field(gt=0)

class EvalSummary(BaseModel):
    generated: str                                          # ISO date
    judge_model: str                                        # provenance, e.g. "gemini-2.5-flash"
    corpus: Corpus
    aggregate: Aggregate
    per_ipo: dict[str, Aggregate] = {}                      # only IPOs with a real gold set
    report: str                                             # path to the .md

    @field_validator("judge_model")
    @classmethod
    def _named_judge(cls, v: str) -> str:                   # honesty invariant: never a figure without a named judge
        if not v:
            raise ValueError("judge_model is required for provenance")
        return v
```
**Framework integration:** DeepEval's `GeminiModel.generate(prompt, schema)` already accepts a Pydantic `schema` and returns a validated instance — that is how DeepEval extracts claims/verdicts reliably (it uses `google-genai` structured output / `instructor` under the hood). For the runner's **own** artifact you never ask an LLM: you construct `EvalSummary(...)` from computed floats and `.model_dump_json(indent=2)`. LLM output is validated inside DeepEval; the committed artifact is validated by your model. **Retry logic:** DeepEval already retries malformed judge JSON internally; add one app-level `tenacity` retry around `metric.measure()` for transient 429/5xx, then fall back to the `-1` "not measured" sentinel and log the judge `reason` to the `.md`. Surface (fail the runner) only when a **deterministic** metric can't compute — never on a judge miss.

### Async-First Design

`FaithfulnessMetric` defaults to `async_mode=True` and runs per-claim verdicts concurrently via `a_generate`; `evaluate()` / `assert_test()` manage their own event loop. **The one mistake:** invoking the async path (or `asyncio.run(...)`) from inside an already-running loop — a Streamlit callback or Jupyter cell — which raises `RuntimeError: This event loop is already running`. The eval runner is a plain `python scripts/run_eval.py` script, so it is safe; just never trigger a judge call from the Streamlit render path (the design already forbids live judge calls at page load — the UI only READS the committed JSON). On free-tier Gemini prefer `async_mode=False` (or `deepeval test run -n 1`): concurrency bursts hit the RPM ceiling → 429s. **Stream vs await:** always **await** the full judge result here — you need the complete `score` + `reason` to validate and write the JSON; there is no user-facing streaming (it is a batch/CI job).

### Prompt Engineering Discipline

You do not hand-write the judge prompt — DeepEval owns the `FaithfulnessMetric` template (a claim-extraction system prompt + a per-claim verdict prompt). If you must tune it, override via `evaluation_template=FaithfulnessTemplate(...)` rather than mutating the question, so the gold-set `question` stays pure user input that matches production queries. **Few-shot:** faithfulness is a claim-verdict task, not generation — no few-shot needed. Treat the gold set (`tests/eval/gold_set.jsonl`) as the "few-shot" of the *suite*: inline, committed, version-controlled — never dynamically retrieved at eval time (reproducibility). **Token budget:** bound the judge's output generation where the wrapper allows (verify the exact `GeminiModel` generation-config kwarg in the DeepEval docs) — an unbounded verdict burns free-tier quota. Never leave output length unbounded in the committed suite.

### Context Window Management

RAG-eval specific: the judge sees only the **top-5 reranked chunks** (already truncated to ~500 chars in `run_eval.py`). If a gold question's expected sources span beyond the top-k, that is a recall miss the **deterministic** `recall@k` catches — do not paper over it by feeding the judge extra context. Truncation/reranking already happened upstream in the agent; the eval faithfully replays that same window. **Do NOT summarize** context before judging — summarization changes what "supported by context" means and detaches faithfulness from the agent's real retrieval. Pass raw reranked chunk text, identical to what the generate node consumed.

### Cost and Latency Budget

**Per-run:** faithfulness fans out ~1 claim-extraction call + N per-claim verdict calls **per question**; for the 13-question Swiggy set budget ~30–60 Gemini calls per full eval run. On free-tier `gemini-2.5-flash` (~1500 RPD, $0) the dollar cost is $0 — the **RPD/RPM ceiling is the real budget**. A single full run is well under 1500 RPD, but back-to-back CI runs plus the opt-in pytest lane can exhaust the per-minute limit. **Caching, two layers:** (1) DeepEval local cache `deepeval test run -c` (exact-match: an identical `(test_case, metric)` skips the judge entirely — ideal when only deterministic code changed); (2) because the UI reads the committed `eval_summary.json` at render, page views have effectively infinite semantic caching — **zero per-view judge cost** (the design's P19 demo-safety). **Cheaper-model routing:** `gemini-2.5-flash` *is* the cheap tier (vs 2.5-pro) — keep the judge on Flash; reserve Pro only for an occasional offline RAGAS cross-check second opinion. Deterministic `recall@k` / `citation_accuracy` cost $0 and run on every CI — push as much of the release gate onto them as possible and keep the LLM judge a reported signal, not a per-commit cost.

## 5. Evaluation Strategy

> Turns the Section 1b domain rubric ingredients into measurable, tooled criteria. Governing rule: **deterministic metrics hard-gate; the LLM-judge is reported-with-a-target; every headline figure is honestly presented (P10) and backed by a human spot-check.**

### Dimensions

| Dimension | Rubric (Pass / Fail) | Measurement | Priority | Gate posture |
|-----------|----------------------|-------------|----------|--------------|
| **Faithfulness / groundedness** (every claim traceable to a retrieved DRHP span) | **PASS:** every number and factual claim in the answer is supported by the top-5 reranked DRHP chunks the agent *actually retrieved* — nothing paraphrased into existence. **FAIL:** a plausible claim absent from the retrieved context — fabricated statistic, invented risk, or "answered from prior knowledge" (P18). | **LLM Judge** — DeepEval `FaithfulnessMetric` on Gemini 2.5 Flash (`temperature=0`, `include_reason=True`, `async_mode=False`); `metric.measure()` path (records, never raises). `-1` sentinel when not measured (key unset / rate-limited). | Critical | **REPORTED** with a ≥0.95 target line. **NOT a CI gate** — the LLM-judge is non-deterministic run-to-run; hard-gating it is itself a critical failure mode (flaky gate). 0.95 target is literature-backed for high-stakes financial RAG. |
| **Recall@k** (retrieval completeness on long, repetitive prospectus boilerplate) | **PASS:** for each gold question, the expected page-span(s) appear within the top-k reranked chunks — the reranked window surfaces the *specific* disclosure. **FAIL:** the true source span sits outside top-k; the answer would draw on generic boilerplate rather than the specific disclosure (a recall miss). | **Code** — deterministic span-overlap over page ranges (pure function, no model). Computed at k = 5 / 10 / 30. | High | **HARD GATE at recall@10 ≥ 0.85** (threshold ratified against the spike-measured baseline — set just below real performance, never self-blocking nor trivially low). k=5 / k=30 reported. |
| **Citation-accuracy** (the cited page actually contains the claim — the project's custom metric) | **PASS:** the cited page/span resolves to a DRHP location that genuinely contains the stated claim. **FAIL:** correct fact, wrong attribution — cites the right section ("Risk Factors") but the wrong page, or a page that does not contain the claim ("citation hallucination"). | **Code** — deterministic span-overlap ("did the cited page actually contain the claim"). | Critical | **HARD GATE at ≥ 0.95** — a citation-accuracy regression is a hard CI failure (CLAUDE.md project rule; 0.95+ literature-backed for high-stakes financial RAG). |
| **Numeric fidelity** (units + magnitude on Indian financial figures — *sub-dimension*) | **PASS:** figures preserve the DRHP's units and scale — lakh vs crore, % of revenue, subscription ×-times — for the right fiscal period. **FAIL:** a unit slip (crore↔lakh), number transposition, or a prior-year figure returned for a current-year question. A wrong number is worse than an honest hedge. | **Code** — the existing `compute_numeric_faithfulness` (via `agent.nodes.cite_check`), unchanged. | Critical | **HARD GATE at ≥ 0.95** — existing gate, unchanged. Folded in here as a noted sub-dimension (see Recommendation below). |
| **Honest, non-prescriptive presentation** (P10 — the harness's own dimension) | **PASS:** every headline figure carries provenance (n, IPO, date, judge model) + a link to the committed report + an interpretation paragraph; a low score and a high score render identically; a reported-not-gating metric is labelled as such. **FAIL:** a metric shown without interpretation / failure analysis; a "reported" metric laundered as a gate; a per-IPO number for an IPO with no real gold set; any red/green / badge / severity / verdict styling. | **Human** (≥50-example spot-check — the non-negotiable calibration step) **+ Code** (honesty-invariant render lint — see Section 6). | Critical | **GATED by `/gsd-eval-review`** (P10 governing gate) — not a numeric threshold, a pass/fail review that must pass for real. |

**Recommendation — numeric fidelity as its own Section-5 row:** For 6.1, keep numeric fidelity as a *noted sub-dimension* riding the existing `numeric_faithfulness ≥ 0.95` release gate rather than a standalone metric row. Rationale: it already has a live, reproducible hard gate, and isolating crore↔lakh / fiscal-period slips as their own scored metric requires the gold set to carry *labelled* per-question unit + period expectations, which the 13-Q Swiggy set does not yet encode. Recommend promoting it to a first-class Section-5 row in a later phase once the gold set labels expected-unit and expected-period per numeric question (this is where the CLAUDE.md "tables as a separate structured index path" work pays off in eval).

**Recommendation — faithfulness stays reported, not asserted:** Confirmed. The deterministic `citation_accuracy` and `recall@10` carry the release gate; DeepEval faithfulness renders a ≥0.95 *target line* and its judge `reason` strings are committed to the `.md` report for auditability. This keeps the CI lane reproducible and pushes as much of the gate as possible onto zero-cost deterministic checks.

## 6. Guardrails

> The real-time "user-facing" surface here is the **inline eval figure** rendered on IPO pages (read from the committed `eval_summary.json`, never a live judge call). Online guardrails are therefore the **honesty-invariant compliance checks** that run at render/emit time — the honesty invariant is a *guardrail*, not merely a UI convention. Kept minimal (each adds latency / surface).

### Online (Real-Time)

| Guardrail | Trigger | Intervention |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| **Honesty-invariant render lint** | An eval figure is about to render with any red/green / badge / severity / verdict CSS class, prescriptive phrasing, or *without* its full provenance string (n, IPO, date, judge model) + report link. | **Block** (fail-closed) — suppress the figure rather than surface it undecorated or verdict-styled. A low score and a high score must render identically. |
| **Per-IPO fabrication guard** | A per-IPO figure is requested for an IPO whose key is absent from the `per_ipo` map (no real gold set). | **Block** the per-IPO number — render the system-level figure only. Never a per-IPO number without a real gold set. |
| **Provenance / schema validation** | `eval_summary.json` fails the pinned Pydantic `EvalSummary` schema, or `judge_model` is empty. | **Block** the surface — never show a figure without a named judge (the `_named_judge` validator enforces this at write time; the render re-checks at read time). |
| **Live-judge-call guard (P19 demo-safety)** | Any DeepEval / Gemini judge call is attempted from the Streamlit render path. | **Block** — the UI only *reads* the committed JSON; zero per-view judge cost/latency. No `asyncio.run` from the render loop. |
| **Banned-token / prescriptive-language scrub** (existing TRUST-02) | Prescriptive phrasing ("subscribe / avoid / buy / sell / target / recommend") appears in any surfaced text. | **Block / scrub** — already live; noted so the eval surface explicitly inherits it (SEBI informational/educational boundary). |

## 7. Production Monitoring

**Tracing Tool:** **Langfuse Cloud (free tier)** — locked, already in use in `app/observability/` (EVAL-05). **Not Arize Phoenix.** The **no-op fallback is preserved**: with `LANGFUSE_PUBLIC_KEY` / `LANGFUSE_SECRET_KEY` unset (local dev, CI without secrets) the agent runs unchanged, pinned by an integration test. 6.1 enriches each trace with cost / latency / tool-call counts and promotes the failure-mode taxonomy to Langfuse **custom scores**. Short-lived scripts (the eval runner, CI) must call `langfuse.flush()` before exit or scores are silently dropped (Section 3, Pitfall 6).

**Key Metrics to Track:**
- **Cost per trace** — Gemini token cost (free-tier is $0, but tracked because the real budget is the ~1500 RPD / per-minute RPM ceiling).
- **Latency per trace and per node** — retrieve / rerank / generate / cite_check.
- **Tool-call count per trace.**
- **Failure-mode custom score** — `retrieval_miss / cite_check_fail / judge_flag / crash`, extending the existing four refusal reasons (`low_retrieval_score / unsupported_claim / banned_token / infrastructure_error`).
- **Faithfulness custom score** (optional) — written back per trace to cross-link the eval figure to the live trace.

**Alert Thresholds** (developer-review signals — single-dev portfolio, not a paging rotation):
- Gemini free-tier usage approaching **~1500 RPD** or hitting the per-minute ceiling (429 bursts) → throttle, keep `async_mode=False`, add `tenacity` backoff.
- `cite_check_fail` or `retrieval_miss` rate rising **above the spike baseline** → investigate (these are the Critical citation/recall modes surfacing live).
- **p95 latency regression** on retrieve/rerank.
- Any **`crash`** failure-mode score → immediate review.
- **Langfuse flush failure** (scores/traces dropped) → verify `flush()` is called in the runner/CI path.

**Smart Sampling Strategy** (how the P10 ≥50-example human review selects interactions — weight toward concerning signals, not a uniform sample):
- Prioritize traces carrying a **failure-mode custom score** (`cite_check_fail / judge_flag / retrieval_miss / crash`), a **low judge-faithfulness `reason`**, a **low retrieval score**, a **refusal**, or **unusual latency / answer length**.
- Manually review **≥50 gold examples** (answer vs cited span vs retrieved context) to (a) confirm the reported figures are real, (b) calibrate the DeepEval judge to **≥0.7 correlation**, and (c) seed the failure gallery. This is the **non-negotiable calibration step before any figure is surfaced** — the human check that keeps the harness out of evaluation theater (P10).
- Watch for **signal–metric divergence**: when the failure-mode distribution or user signals diverge from the reported scores, investigate manually — it is the early warning that the metric or the gold set has a coverage gap (e.g. the RPT-attribution gap flagged in Section 5).

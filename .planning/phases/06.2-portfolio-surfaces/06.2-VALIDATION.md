*Status: ⬜ pending · ✅ green · ❌ red · ⚠️ flaky*

**What each test *samples* / minimum trustworthy coverage:**
- **Schema test** samples **every** entry (not a subset) — count, surface-coverage, and per-entry key/postmortem/status are cheap and must be exhaustive; a sampled subset could let a malformed entry through.
- **AST test** samples the **whole module source** (finite import graph) — full, not sampled.
- **Render test** must exercise **both** an `open` and a `mitigated` entry to prove status is text-only (mirrors `test_eval_inline.py::test_value_invariant_styling`), plus the empty-state path.
- **Parity test** asserts **each** headline number independently (citation, recall, faithfulness sentinel, coverage) — one combined assertion would mask a single drifted figure.
- **Self-contained test** greps for `https?://` in `src=`/`href=`/`url(` positions across the **whole** file, not a sample.
